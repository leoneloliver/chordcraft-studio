How it works

From chords to a playing band in three steps.

Type your chords

Add the chords of your song to a section. Not sure how a chord is played? Look it up in the chord finder.

Press play

Four beats of count-in, then the band starts. Choose the groove, the tempo and who is in the band.

Play along and save

Loop the tricky bar, slow it down, and save the song. Next time it is one click away.
